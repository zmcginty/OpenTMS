Design #1 Power Electronics

main SCR switch;
    model;      TT570N16KOF
    description;    1.6kV dual series diode module (3.2kV for whole module). 600A continuous, 17kA surge

Gate drivers for all SCR modules; 
    Whatever decent-size mosfet I could find, low-side switching a 1:1 isolation transformer which has one or multiple outputs which go to the SCR gate inputs.
    Pulse-width and edge-rates don't really seem to matter much here for the SCRs. there's no turn-off spike.... life's easy. Just throw is a pulse shorter than the time it takes to discharge your capacitor (because the SCR is going to latch and FULLY discharge your cap.)
